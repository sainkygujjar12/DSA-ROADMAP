# Company logo sources

These assets identify their respective companies. Brand marks remain the property
of their owners. Downloaded and checked on 2026-10-07.

- Website icons: domains are recorded in `src/data/companyLogoDomains.json`;
  retrieved through `https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=DOMAIN&sz=128`.
- SVG brand marks for Audible, ByteDance, Cisco, eBay, Intel, Meta, Nutanix,
  Qualtrics, Samsung, and Twitter: exported from the project's installed
  React Icons / Simple Icons / Font Awesome collections.
- IBM: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@9.21.0/icons/ibm.svg
- AppDynamics: https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos/blob/main/logos/appdynamics-icon.svg
- Cohesity: https://static.cdnlogo.com/logos/c/55/cohesity.svg
- Cruise: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cruise_logo.svg
- Triplebyte: https://seeklogo.com/vector-logo/508357/triplebyte

`src/data/companyLogos.json` selects the reviewed asset for each company.
Historical Twitter and Cruise marks match the names in the question dataset.
Website redirects were not used as logos for AppDynamics or Cruise.
The website-icon downloader does not replace the reviewed SVG overrides.
